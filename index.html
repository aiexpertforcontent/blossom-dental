Featured Patient Guide

Oral Health Awareness: Clinical Survey Report

Clinical Summary: Based on clinical research published in the Indian Journal of Dentistry, this educational report uncovers the real daily habits of dental patients and explains why ordinary brushing is often not enough to prevent tooth decay and gum disease.

Key Survey Findings:

  • Night-Time Brushing Gap: Over 90% of patients brush their teeth once every morning, but only 25% brush at night. This leaves food particles and bacteria free to attack tooth enamel for 8 uninterrupted hours of sleep.
  • The Flossing Deficiency: Over 80% do not clean between their teeth. A standard toothbrush only cleans 60% of tooth surfaces; the remaining 40% between teeth requires floss or small interdental brushes.
  • Whole-Body Health Connection: More than 56% were unaware that long-term gum inflammation allows oral bacteria to enter the bloodstream, directly impacting heart and metabolic health.

3 Simple Steps to Protect Your Smile:

  1. Brush gently with a soft-bristle brush for two minutes every morning and every night before bed.
  2. Clean between teeth daily with floss or an interdental brush to stop hidden gum bleeding.
  3. Visit your dentist twice a year for gentle ultrasonic cleaning and early check-ups.
